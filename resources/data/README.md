Downloaded 2026-10-10 from https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/tree/master/geojson . Map uses an equirectangular projection. Regional aliases use a representative city centre, not an application address. Unmatched labels are never assigned invented coordinates.

City/regency boundaries: geoBoundaries gbOpen IDN ADM2 (2020), BPS / World Food Programme / OCHA ROAP, CC BY 3.0 IGO. https://www.geoboundaries.org/api/current/gbOpen/IDN/ADM2/ . Simplified geometry projected to local SVG coordinates; no municipal borders are invented. Jakarta aggregates its five mainland city units.
